Flagship event

Christmas Eve Goodies Share

On 24 December 2026 we travel to a selected seaside town in the Central Region to share goodies with impoverished children who otherwise have nothing to be happy about this Christmas. Fishing families here lose income in the lean season, and for most of these children Christmas passes like any other hungry day.

We will hand out hot meals and treat bags, gift new school supplies for the January term, and run a pop-up health post with volunteer nurses for basic screening and referrals. Every child under 16 in the community is welcome.
