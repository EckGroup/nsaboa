About us

Built for the villages that never make the funding shortlist

Nkosuo Mma Foundation is a volunteer-led non-profit working with children under 16 in the remote inland and coastal communities of Ghana’s Central Region.

Our story

We kept noticing the same pattern: nearly every non-profit we admired concentrated its work in Accra, the Eastern Region and Ashanti. Those places need help too — but the maps of who gets served left whole districts of the Central Region blank.

So five friends pooled their own money, borrowed a pickup, and drove to villages where children shared one pencil between three desks and had never been weighed by a health worker. That first trip became this foundation.

Mission

To give every child under 16 in the remote Central Region the three things that change a life: learning materials, basic health care and a route to finish school.

How we work

We visit with community elders and school heads first, verify need, then deliver in person — supplies each term, screening clinics with volunteer nurses, and scholarship sponsorship for the brightest pupils.

Global framework

Our work maps directly to the UN SDGs and WHO child health standards

SDG 1

No Poverty

Direct relief to households living below the line.

SDG 3

Good Health & Well-being

WHO-aligned child screening, deworming and referrals.

SDG 4

Quality Education

Supplies and scholarships so no bright child drops out.

SDG 10

Reduced Inequalities

Serving villages left out of the Accra-Ashanti-Eastern focus.

We follow WHO guidance on integrated management of childhood illness, growth monitoring and immunisation follow-up during every village check-up, and we report our reach against the SDG indicators for under-16 school participation.
